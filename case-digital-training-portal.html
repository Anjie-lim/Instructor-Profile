Digital Training Portal

A web-based training concept structured around introduction, instruction, exploration, practice, and assessment.

Digital Learning Learning Experience Interactive Training Prototyping Information Architecture AI-Assisted Development
← Back to portfolio

Challenge

Training content needs to do more than present information. Learners need a clear path through the material, opportunities to examine examples, and structured ways to practise before being assessed.

The portal concept was designed to bring instructional content, visual examples, interactive exploration, and assessment into one learner-facing experience.

My Role

  • Designed the structure and learner flow for a web-based training experience.
  • Organized content into progressive sections supporting orientation, learning, exploration, practice, and assessment.
  • Designed learner-facing information architecture and interaction concepts.
  • Developed the training prototype with AI-assisted tools and iterated on the learning experience.
  • Connected instructional explanations with visual examples, activities, and knowledge checks.

Learning Approach

  • Orient: establish the task, purpose, and learning expectations.
  • Teach: present the core concepts and criteria in manageable sections.
  • Explore: let learners inspect examples and identify relevant features.
  • Practise: move learners from recognition to application.
  • Assess: check understanding through structured knowledge checks.

Deliverables

  • Learner flow and information architecture
  • Interactive training portal prototype
  • Visual example / inspection components
  • Guided activities
  • Knowledge-check concepts
  • Learner-facing navigation and interface structure
