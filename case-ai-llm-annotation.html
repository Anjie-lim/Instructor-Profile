Challenge

Annotators working on AI evaluation tasks need to interpret detailed guidelines consistently and apply evaluation standards across varied examples and scenarios.

The training work focused on turning complex task requirements into practical guidance that analysts could understand, apply, discuss during calibration, and use when making evaluation decisions.

My Role

  • Developed structured training resources for AI/LLM annotation and evaluation workflows.
  • Translated complex guidelines into practical instructions, examples, scenarios, and quality-focused guidance.
  • Supported onboarding and knowledge transfer for new team members.
  • Participated in calibration discussions and contributed to improving consistency across evaluators.
  • Contributed to rubrics, decision frameworks, annotation guidance, and quality assurance procedures.

Learning Approach

  • Explain: introduce the task, criteria, and expected standards.
  • Demonstrate: use concrete examples to show how the criteria are applied.
  • Explore: provide varied scenarios that require interpretation and comparison.
  • Calibrate: surface differences in interpretation and align decisions against the guidance.
  • Assess: reinforce understanding through practical evaluation and feedback.

Deliverables

  • Annotation and evaluation training guidance
  • Practical examples and scenarios
  • Decision trees and evaluation frameworks
  • Calibration resources
  • Visual examples for AI-generated image error identification
  • Knowledge-check concepts and trainer-facing guidance
