Challenge

Digital learning tools need to reflect sound instructional structure, not simply place existing content on a screen.

These projects explored how learner workflows, practice modes, assessment, and guided interaction can be organized into technology-supported learning experiences.

My Role

  • Developed concepts for digital learning tools supporting reading practice, assessment, learner interaction, and guided practice.
  • Designed learner workflows and instructional modes.
  • Structured practice activities and learning content around intended learner actions.
  • Applied classroom and instructional experience to technology-supported learning concepts.
  • Used AI-assisted tools to prototype and iterate on digital learning experiences.

Learning Approach

  • Define the learner task and intended outcome.
  • Structure the learning flow around clear instructional stages.
  • Provide guided practice before independent application.
  • Use assessment or feedback to support learner progress.
  • Iterate the experience based on instructional logic and usability.

Deliverables

  • Digital learning tool concepts
  • Learner workflows
  • Assessment and practice modes
  • Guided practice activities
  • Content structures and instructional sequences
  • AI-assisted prototypes
